Content Cluster Analysis
🏙 JB Destination Content
Highest volume undertap
Approx. 35 articles covering Johor Bahru destinations (malls, food, activities, transport). Combined impressions: ~4.5M. Average CTR: ~1.5%. Top gaps: Train to JB (508K imp/1.3%), Mid Valley Southkey (361K/1.25%), JB Food Guide (298K/1.59%). The cluster collectively ranks positions 5–9 — title rewrites with year + intent modifier would be highest-ROI action.
→ Batch title rewrite: "X in JB 2026 — Complete Guide for Singaporeans" pattern · est. +15K clicks/mo
🇨🇳 China Payment Articles
High volume, improving
WeChat Pay (493K imp/1.72%) and Alipay (506K imp/1.19%) are the two highest-impression low-CTR pages outside JB content. Positions 7–9 limit CTR structurally — content refresh + backlink push to positions 4–6 would double CTR. Note: WeChat Pay CTR at 1.72% is approaching the 2% threshold — it may graduate out of the gap list next quarter with position improvement.
→ Update articles for 2026 (WeChat 10 acceptance, Alipay international) + internal links from JB articles
💱 Currency / Exchange Rate Pages
URL split causing waste
Two competing URLs: /blog/from-sgd-to-yen/ (423K imp/0.56% CTR, pos 9.1) and /blog/youtrip-exchange-rates/ (180K imp/3.67%). The SGD-to-yen page has massive impressions but poor CTR — likely because title doesn't match user intent for this query set. The new /sgd-to-yen/ URL (not yet indexed) should absorb this traffic once the canonical issue is fixed. Until then, optimise the /blog/ page title.
→ Fix /sgd-to-yen/ indexing first (see Indexing page) · then merge signals via canonical/301
📅 Seasonal / Event Content
Single biggest gap: 0.11% CTR
Singapore Public Holiday 2026 (/blog/singapore-public-holiday-2026/) has 331,831 impressions and only 0.11% CTR (365 clicks). This is the worst CTR in the dataset and was also flagged in the July 2026 audit. The page likely ranks for informational queries but the title/meta fails to differentiate from government sources. Adding YouTrip-specific value ("When to Use YouTrip for the 2026 Long Weekends") in the title + rich results could dramatically improve CTR.
→ Rewrite title/meta · add FAQ schema · recommend 2026 public holiday travel tips angle · est. +5–10K clicks/mo
