Diagnosis — What's Happening
🔎

Old /blog/ URLs are indexed · New canonical URLs are not

GSC traffic data shows the old /blog/ URLs are still receiving impressions and clicks (e.g., /blog/youtrip-exchange-rates/ gets 6,619 clicks). The new clean URLs (e.g., /youtrip-exchange-rates/) are "unknown to Google" — either they do not exist yet, lack a canonical tag pointing to themselves, or redirect back to the old URLs. Until the new URLs are crawlable and self-canonical, Google will not index them.

⚠️

Mobile usability verdict unspecified for 5 pages

When a page is "unknown to Google," mobile usability cannot be assessed. This means we cannot confirm mobile friendliness for these key pages — fixing the indexing issue is a prerequisite for getting mobile usability data.

Fix Steps — Dev Ticket
  1. Confirm whether /sg/sgd-to-yen/ and the other 4 new URLs are live and returning 200 (not redirecting or 404ing).
  2. If pages are live: check that <link rel="canonical" href="https://www.you.co/sg/sgd-to-yen/"> is present and self-referencing — not pointing back to /blog/.
  3. If pages redirect to /blog/: flip the redirect direction — old /blog/ URLs should 301 to the new clean URLs, not the reverse.
  4. Submit the 5 new URLs for (re-)indexing via GSC "Request Indexing" button immediately after confirming.
  5. Update internal links site-wide: replace all /blog/youtrip-exchange-rates/ hrefs with /youtrip-exchange-rates/ etc.
  6. Add these 5 URLs to sitemap.xml if not already included.
Priority: Do this before any other SEO work. All CWV fixes, content optimisations, and schema additions on these pages are invisible to Google until they are indexed.
