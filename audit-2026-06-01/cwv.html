Key Findings

All 6 pages fail mobile LCP Mobile LCP ranges from 6.0s to 26.9s across all audited pages. The "Good" threshold is 2.5s. This is a site-wide infrastructure issue, not a page-by-page problem.
Desktop performance is better but inconsistent Desktop scores range from 62 (Homepage) to 90 (Public Holidays). Homepage desktop TBT is 410ms — above the 200ms Good threshold.
CLS is excellent across all pages All pages score CLS of 0 or near-0. Layout stability is not an issue — focus effort on LCP and TBT instead.
Content pages perform better than Homepage on mobile The About YouTrip page (62), Travel Insurance (58), and Public Holidays (65) score higher than the Homepage (42) on mobile — suggesting the homepage has unique rendering bottlenecks.
